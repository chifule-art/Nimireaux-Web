III • THE ATELIERS

Chilled Rollers. Surgical Extraction.

Inside our climate-controlled processing halls, grain passes through graduated stages of pneumatic destoning, precision tempering, and multi-break chilled cast-iron rollers. We gently shave the endosperm while preserving its vital starches and separating the fibrous bran with zero thermal stress.
