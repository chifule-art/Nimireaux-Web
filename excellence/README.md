# 🌾 Nimireaux Uganda Limited — The Excellence Edition (Vertical Scroll Experience)

A fluid, continuous vertical-scrolling web experience for **Nimireaux Uganda Limited**, engineered with rich imperial colors, regal typography, and a touch of excellence.

> **Tagline:** *"PURE NUTRITION. POWERING POSSIBILITIES."*  
> **Core Services:** *Grain Processing and other Milling Services*

---

## 🏛️ Key Architectural Features

1. **Natural, Fluid Vertical Scroll Architecture**:
   - Continuous scroll flow down the page with smooth browser-native scrolling (`scroll-behavior: smooth`).
   - Real-time reading progress bar at the top of the viewport (`scroll-progress-bar`).
   - Sticky navigation header that transforms on scroll with frosted glass blur.
   - Smooth IntersectionObserver scroll-reveal animations (`animate-on-scroll`).

2. **Rich & Regal Color Palette**:
   - **Imperial Forest Green** (`#0a2114`, `#123c23`, `#194e2e`) representing Ugandan agronomy, growth, and industrial vitality.
   - **Tuscan Harvest Gold & Brushed Brass** (`#c99a3e`, `#dfb15b`, `#f3cf7a`) honoring pure cereal grain wealth.
   - **Warm Alabaster & Silky Ivory** (`#fcfaf6`, `#f7f3ea`) for clean, pristine editorial readability.
   - **Velvet Dark Emerald Accents** (`#07150d`) in the hero and footer sections.

3. **Complete Section Flow**:
   - **Hero Section**: Full-width entrance with golden harvest backdrop, ambient particle canvas, royal insignia, metrics ribbon, and direct CTAs.
   - **Heritage & Provenance**: The soil and farming cooperatives of Masindi, Bunyoro, and Acholi with contract farming stats.
   - **Core Services**: 4-column cards for Commercial Maize Milling, Wheat & Specialty Grains, Custom Toll Milling, and Livestock Feed By-Products.
   - **The 5-Stage Process Flow**: Visual pipeline from Spectrographic Assay, Multi-Screen Destoning, Hydrothermal Conditioning, Corrugated Roller Milling, to UNBS Bagging.
   - **Certified Flour Products & Nutrition Label**: Dual layout with interactive product picker and real-time UNBS nutrition facts sheet.
   - **Industrial Batch Yield Calculator**: Embedded live calculator with batch tonnage slider (10 to 2,000 MT), commodity radio buttons, and one-click transfer to RFQ.
   - **Quality Certifications**: UNBS Q-Mark, 0.0 ppb Aflatoxin zero-tolerance guarantee, Non-GMO verification, and HACCP standards.
   - **The Official Business Card & RFQ Salon**: Authentic digital replica of the printed business card with clickable emails and phone hotlines, WhatsApp VIP link, and commercial quotation form.
   - **Architectural Corporate Footer**: Full sitemap, facility working hours, registered office details, and legal compliance.
