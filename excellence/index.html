ENGINEERING PROTOCOL

The Science of Purity: 5 Precision Stages.

Every kilogram of grain undergoes a calibrated sequence of mechanical, hydrothermal, and optical operations to isolate pure vitreous starch while enriching micronutrients.

01

Intake & Spectrographic Assay

Grain is sampled via automated pneumatic spears. Real-time NIR spectroscopy tests for moisture (≤12.8%), test weight, and immediate aflatoxin clearance.

0.0 ppb Aflatoxin Filter
02

Multi-Screen Destoning & Scour

Vibratory high-capacity destoners, magnetic separators, and rotary aspirators eliminate 99.95% of stones, chaff, dust, and metallic vitreous impurities.

99.95% Foreign Matter Purged
03

Hydrothermal Conditioning

Computer-metered water misting tempers the grain for 12–16 hours, elevating the husk elasticity so it separates effortlessly from the delicate endosperm starch.

14.2% Optimal Milling Moisture
04

Corrugated Roller Milling

Chilled cast-iron differential break rollers shave the grain with surgical precision. High-speed multi-deck plansifters classify pure flour down to 120 μm.

Sub-Micron Starch Preservation
05

UNBS Fortification & Bagging

Gravimetric micro-feeders infuse Vitamin A, Zinc, Iron, and Folic Acid according to Uganda standards before automated hermetic packing in food-grade bags.

Mandatory Vitamin Enrichment
